Privacy Policy

Sarvocch Microcare Foundation ("the Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy and confidentiality of individuals who access this website (https://sarvocchfinance.com) and contact our organisation.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting an online loan enquiry, filing a customer grievance, or sending a direct message. This information may include:

  • Full Name
  • Contact Number (Mobile / Landline)
  • Email Address
  • State, District, or Residential Location
  • Requested Financial Product and Amount
  • Details regarding business purpose or grievance description

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used strictly for legitimate institutional purposes, including:

  • Contacting you to process your financial inquiries or loan applications.
  • Investigating and resolving customer grievances according to our Fair Practice Code.
  • Customer service and communication regarding our branch network in Bhopal and Pipariya.
  • Fulfilling legal, statutory, and regulatory record-keeping obligations under applicable Indian law.

3. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Sarvocch Microcare Foundation adopts reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect customer data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party commercial marketers.

4. Disclosure Under Legal Obligation

We may disclose submitted data if required to do so by applicable law, statutory authorities, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines, Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), judicial orders, or law enforcement agencies.
